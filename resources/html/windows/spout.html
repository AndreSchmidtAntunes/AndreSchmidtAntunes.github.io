SPOUT

Spout is a university project about a spider on a pilgrimage to the top of the neverending water spout.

PROCESS

To make this project, I was tasked with writing a short story and to develop a playable prototype for it. In total the project was given 4 weeks from planning to final product.

I created my own 3D sprite component to render sprites in 3D space, providing control of how the sprite follows the camera, the z-offset of the sprite in 3D space, and general 2D transformation values.

I also created all the materials and textures for this project using shader-graph.
